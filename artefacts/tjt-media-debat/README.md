## 1. Overview & Editorial Charter
**Tribune des Jeunes Talents (TJT)** is a high-level civic media format and podcast platform designed to cultivate dialectical debate, democratic deliberation, and fact-grounded public policy discussions among the emerging generation of African leaders.

### Core Principles
1. **Evidence-Based Argumentation:** Every thesis submitted during deliberations must reference empirical data, national legislation, or recognized peer-reviewed sources.
2. **Dialectical Pluralism:** Confronting technocratic optimism with legal, socio-economic, and constitutional realities.
3. **Structured Non-Violent Deliberation:** Applying the Oxford Union and parliamentary debate standards tailored to West African civic contexts.
